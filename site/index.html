From screenshot to paste.

Find an earlier image

Take a screenshot or copy an image. Open BarCut and click a thumbnail to copy it again. BarCut keeps the latest ten images on your Mac.

Copy with the keyboard

Press ⇧⌘2 to copy the newest image. Keep Shift and Command held and press 2 or an arrow key to choose an older one. Release to copy.

Crop and mark up

Draw arrows and boxes, type a label, or crop to the part you want. BarCut copies the result after every edit, ready to paste.

Install BarCut.

For macOS 26 or later on an Apple silicon Mac. BarCut works locally and makes no network requests.

↓ Download for Mac
  1. Open the downloaded disk image and drag BarCut to Applications.
  2. Open BarCut from Applications. Its icon appears in the menu bar.
  3. If macOS asks for access to your Desktop, click Allow so BarCut can find new screenshots in the default screenshot folder.
